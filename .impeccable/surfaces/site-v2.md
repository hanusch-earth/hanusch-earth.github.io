# Surface brief: hanusch.earth (v2, client template refined)

Mode: Read (academic profile site; visitors look up specifics). Home is Read with a light Persuade layer.
Target: `site/` (Astro, static build in `site/dist/`).

## History

Directions A–D (Horizon Hall, Earth's Record, Deep-Time Chart, Planetary Section) were rejected by the client on 2026-10-18 as "too planetary". The client supplied his own AI-built site (`~/Downloads/hanusch-earth-website/website`). Its content is binding; its look was refined, not replaced (refinement preserves).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet scholarly index on a near-black ground: the work (books, lists, dates) carries the page; no planetary imagery or decoration. Refuses the illustrated hero and the card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #0B0D0F, text #F4F2ED, body #D2D0CA, meta #8A9096, hairlines #24272B / #3A3E43. Neon #D7FF00 only in the logo mark, the active nav underline, and one highlight per page (home: the new-book marker). Space Grotesk 300/400 for display and UI, Source Sans 3 for running text. Two-column sections: sticky sentence-case heading left (3/12), content right (9/12). Dated rows with tabular years shown once per group. Covers with a hairline and slight dimming. Official "Horizon" mark (disc with cut).

STORY: An organiser or journalist sees who he is (name, portrait, role) in the first viewport, finds the current book, the three research areas, recent media, and the email within one scroll, and reaches CV, publications and contact in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home, desktop): light 104px name left, portrait 4:5 at right overlapping the horizon rule, role and one-sentence intro below the rule, three quick links (CV and biography, Publications, Email). At 600px and below the portrait moves above the name instead of overlapping. Sub pages: huge light title, horizon rule, in-page index left, lead text and photo right.

FORM: client template, refined. seed: none (direction supplied by the client; no concept roll applies).

Signature interaction and motion: the horizon rule under the title draws in once on load (1.1s expo-out, off under reduced motion); filters on Publications and Media with counts that re-group year labels.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
